About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Theoretical Computer Science at the Lebanese American University, working remotely with Prof. Faisal N. Abu-Khzam.

I work in parameterized approximation, parameterized complexity, and graph algorithms. My research studies how structural restrictions on graphs and constraint systems can be exploited to obtain efficient exact, approximation, and preprocessing algorithms.

I received my Ph.D. in Theoretical Computer Science from The Institute of Mathematical Sciences in 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Saket Saurabh. My thesis is titled Parameterized approximation algorithms for Partial Covering and Satisfiability with budget constraints Thesis.
