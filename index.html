About

Researcher in algorithms and complexity

I am a postdoctoral researcher in theoretical computer science. I am currently affiliated with the Lebanese American University as a Postdoctoral Non-resident Faculty member, hosted by Prof. Faisal N. Abu-Khzam, and with The Institute of Mathematical Sciences, Chennai, as a Visiting Scientist.

I received my Ph.D. in Theoretical Computer Science from The Institute of Mathematical Sciences in 2026. My thesis, supervised by Prof. Saket Saurabh, was titled Parameterized approximation algorithms for Partial Covering and Satisfiability with budget constraints.

Research

What I work on

Parameterized Approximation

Designing approximation algorithms whose running time can depend non-polynomially on a parameter while remaining polynomial in the overall input size, especially for covering and satisfiability problems.

Parameterized Complexity

Understanding how structural and combinatorial parameters change the computational complexity of hard problems, and identifying useful tractability boundaries.

Graph Algorithms

Studying graph problems through structural restrictions and parameterized techniques, including domination, coloring, and related optimization problems.
